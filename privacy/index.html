Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Milligo collects no data. What you log stays on your phone. Milligo has no accounts and no server, and it never sends what you log to us or to anyone else. On Android it doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

What stays on your phone

  • Your plan: when it started, how many pouches a day you started from, their strength, your goal, your pace and any pauses.
  • Every pouch you log: when you logged it, when it came out, its strength and its timer.
  • Your weekly spend, your currency and your savings goal, including the name you give it.
  • Your settings, such as the pouch timer, notifications and theme.

Milligo also reads your phone's language and region, to show the right language and suggest a currency. Those stay on the phone too. Notifications are scheduled on the phone, with no outside service.

No ads, no tracking, no backups

Milligo has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind, and nothing from another company inside it does those things. Nobody else gets your data. Milligo also tells your phone to leave its data out of backups, such as iCloud and Google, so it doesn't end up in a cloud account either.

The cost: if you uninstall Milligo or lose your phone, your data is gone, and we can't bring it back.

How your data is protected

Your data sits in Milligo's private storage on the phone, where other apps can't read it, and it never travels over a network. Milligo adds no encryption of its own. It relies on your phone's built-in encryption and screen lock, so a screen lock is worth having. Timer notifications can show on your lock screen and mention your pouch timer. You can turn them off in Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. In Settings, tap Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Either one removes your data from the phone, and there's no other copy for us to delete.

If you email us

Writing to us is your choice, and Milligo works the same if you never do. Contact us in Settings opens your email app with a message to support@milligo.app and two lines filled in: the app version and your phone's system version (for example, "iOS 26.1"). Nothing you've logged is included. You can read and remove those lines first, and nothing goes anywhere unless you send it.

If you do send it, we receive your email address, what you wrote and those two lines, through the email services that deliver it, which may handle them outside the European Union, including in the United States. We use them only to answer you (our legal basis is a legitimate interest in replying to messages we receive), keep them only as long as we need them to help you, and delete them if you ask. We don't sell them or share them with anyone else.

The app stores

Apple and Google run the stores and handle your store account, your download and anything you share with them, under their own privacy policies. They show us overall numbers, such as downloads. If you've agreed on your phone to share data with app developers, they also show us usage statistics, such as how many people still use the app after a week, and crash reports. We use these only to see how Milligo is doing and to fix problems. Milligo adds nothing you log to any of this.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or scripts, and loads nothing from other companies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and may handle them outside the European Union, including in the United States (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). Privacy policy and About in Settings open these pages in your phone's browser. They're the only web pages Milligo opens.

Children

Milligo is meant for adults who already use nicotine pouches, not for children.

Who's responsible, and your rights

Milligo is made by Radosław Lichtarski, who is the data controller for messages you send us. Since the app collects nothing, those messages are the only personal data we could hold. At support@milligo.app you can ask to see, correct or delete it, to get a copy, or to limit how we use it.

You can also object at any time to our using it to reply to you.

And you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If Milligo were ever to send data off your phone, this page would say so before that version of the app is released.
